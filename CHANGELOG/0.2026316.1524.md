### Why the ref steps have to leave the lowering (leg 2's blocker)

`lower.go` collects a plan's `$step.path` references and rewrites them to `${REF_n}` — the only spelling the plan expander (`kit.ExpandOpVars`) reads. Both steps are the **one** application site of the ref grammar `refRe`, and `refRe` is package-private in `workflowkit/expr.go`, where the same grammar serves the expression validator (`Validate`, `RefNames`, `RefIDs`, `RewriteRefs`, `HasRef`) that every pipeline body is judged by. A plugin-side copy of that regex would be a second implementation of one grammar (R2) and would drift from that gate; keeping the grammar here and exporting the two steps keeps it single.

The functions move verbatim — same body, same order, same single pass — and the three call sites are renamed to the exported names.
