## Summary

Final leg of umbrella campaign `opencharly/opencharly#286` (+ the `#290` retire
sweep): add a tailored, thin, harness-neutral `AGENTS.md` signpost to
`opencharly/sdk`, the OpenCharly plugin SDK, and retire its `CLAUDE.md`. The
README was already a good user overview and is kept as-is (this PR does not touch
it).

`AGENTS.md` documents what the repo is (the root serve surface + `kit/`,
`deploykit/`, `buildkit/`, `enginekit/`, `loaderkit/`, `agentkit/`, `vmshared/`,
and the fact that the wire/IR/schema contract lives in the separate
`github.com/opencharly/spec` module), the R0 owning skills
(`/charly-internals:plugin`, `/charly-internals:go`,
`/charly-internals:install-plan`), the real build/validate commands, the
"mechanism not capability" placement rule, the sdk-specific tag scheme, and a
landing pointer — no rule restatement.

**`CLAUDE.md` retire:** the tracked `CLAUDE.md` is deleted, and its 5 live
`CLAUDE.md` tokens in hand-authored source/config are swept to `AGENTS.md`:
`.golangci.yml:1`, `buildkit/capabilities_test.go:12`,
`deploykit/install_build.go:1006` + `:1056`, `deploykit/plan_compile_test.go:398`.
The sweep is comment/config-only (no behavior change).
