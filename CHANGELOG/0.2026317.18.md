# 0.2026317.18 — refactor(workflowkit)!: delete the task-shaped helpers plugin-task now owns

## Summary

**Leg 2 of unit 2's four-leg decomposition** (`TODO.md` 2.1, tracker `opencharly/spec#304`, row 2; unit 1 was the `pipeline` kind). Leg 1 (`opencharly/plugin-task#66`) moved the `kind: task` body into the plugin's own CUE module and brought the four task-shaped helpers HOME; this deletes the SDK's copies so the helpers have ONE home again.

The four legs, and where they stand: **1** `plugin-task` (the body + the helpers + `InputDef`) → **2 this PR** (`sdk` deletes its copies) → **3** `spec` deletes `#Task`/`#TaskValue` → **4** `charly` bumps `plugin-task`/`sdk`/`spec` in lockstep and retargets the three fixtures that name `#TaskValue`.

### What is deleted

| file | why it is `plugin-task`'s |
|---|---|
| `workflowkit/closure.go` (69 lines) + `closure_test.go` (55) | `Closure`/`SortedNames` take `map[string]spec.Task`; both live in `plugin-task`'s `closure.go` since leg 1 |
| `workflowkit/args.go`'s `ResolveArgs` + `MergedEnv` (~43 lines) + their 3 test cases | both take `spec.Task`; both live in `plugin-task`'s `resolve.go` since leg 1 |

**`ResolveTaskDir` STAYS** (with its `TestResolveTaskDir`), and so does `ExpandVars` it calls: both take strings, know no task body, and are shared. `workflowkit` now carries only the KIND-BLIND surface — `PlanRefs`, `RewritePlanRefs`, `RefIDs`, `RefNames`, `Validate`, the `refRe` grammar, `cron.go`, `template.go` and the dir resolution.

### Why they were here, and why that reason is gone

They moved INTO the SDK in `plugin-task` CHANGELOG 2026.277.0711 because the SDK's pipeline lowering was their second caller ("the plugin-task CLI and the workflow lowering share ONE implementation"). Unit 1 moved that lowering into the plugin that owns the pipeline kind, so `sdk` has no caller left — and this repo's own rulebook says `sdk` is a reusable MECHANISM library, "kind-blind and free of capability code". A helper typed on `spec.Task` cannot stay in a kind-blind library once the task body lives with its own plugin. This is not a revert of R3: the second caller no longer exists.

## Change classification

**Breaking change + rollback:** `workflowkit.ResolveArgs`, `workflowkit.MergedEnv`, `workflowkit.Closure` and `workflowkit.SortedNames` disappear from the exported API. Nothing in the materialized org calls them (measured above; `plugin-task` stopped in leg 1), so no consumer breaks at the import level — an OUT-OF-TREE consumer that pinned the SDK and called them would fail to compile, which is the intended hard cutover. Rollback is a revert of this commit; `plugin-task` keeps its own copies either way, so the revert restores duplication rather than breaking a caller.
