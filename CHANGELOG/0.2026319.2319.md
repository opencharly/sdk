## Summary

The **consumer half** of `opencharly/spec` `v0.2026319.2146` (where `#CandyModel.data` became `bytes`), and the **third** field on the pattern `hook` (`spec#331`/`sdk#531`) and `extract` (`spec#332`/`sdk#534`) established. Closes **opencharly/sdk#535**.

**No new mechanism.** Both sides go through the shared crossing the `extract` PR collapsed (`marshalOpaque` + `hasOpaqueSlice` + `HasData`'s unchanged `len()`, `decodeOpaque`), which is why this diff is small: the third field costs one encode, one decode, and two tests.

**The empty-non-nil case is tested explicitly.** `hasOpaqueSlice` keys on `len() > 0`, not `!= nil`, because an empty non-nil slice marshals to `[]` — two non-empty bytes — and `HasData()` (`len(a.m.Data) > 0`) would then answer *true* for a candy that declares no data seeding. That exact bug was caught by review on the `extract` PR; this test exists so it cannot recur here.
