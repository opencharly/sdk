## Summary

Adopts `spec v0.2026289.611` — whose `#CandyModel` gained `skills:` (`opencharly/spec#254`) — and populates it, so a consumer holding only the wire model can answer *"which skills does this candy carry?"* without reaching `cfg.Skills`.

**The asymmetry the field closes, from the reader itself.** *`CollectSkills` already takes `layers map[string]CandyModel` and uses it for `allCandyNames` — but reads the owner association from `cfg.Skills`.* **The wire model was in the signature and not in the answer.**

## The design point that decides the shape

***`CandyModel.Skills` carries the candy's OWNED skill names — a PER-CANDY projection that needs no composition.*** **And that is load-bearing, not pedantic:** `CollectSkills` filters by the composed candy chain because a **LABEL** must stay bounded, and *`skill.cue` says `owner` is "the build-time association that decides which images carry it", while `CollectSkills`'s own doc records that concept candies "are not image layers and never appear in any image's label".* **A candy that no image composes still OWNS its skills.** ***Copying the box filter here would make `charly-internals` own its skills under one box and not under another.***
